Town of Larch Grove: Council Meeting Minutes

Mayor Nadia Brennan, Council members Iris Kaplan, Theo Marsh, Lucia Ferreira and Desmond Hale.

The minutes below cover this year's regular meetings. Approved minutes are posted within two weeks of each meeting.

Regular meeting, January

The Mayor called the meeting to order at 7 p.m. All members were present. The minutes of the previous meeting were approved as written.

Snow removal contract

The current snow removal contract ends in April. Two companies bid. Council member Iris Kaplan asked whether either company had local references. Both did. The council chose the lower bid, which also offers a fourth truck for storms over eight inches.

Park pavilion rental fees

The Parks Director proposed raising the pavilion rental fee from $84 to $107 per day for non-residents, with no change for residents. Council member Lucia Ferreira asked how many rentals came from non-residents last year. The answer was 43. The change was approved.

Speed humps on Orchard Lane

Neighbors presented a petition with 85 signatures asking for speed humps on Maple Avenue. The Police Chief reported an average speed of 31 miles per hour in a 25 zone. The council asked for a traffic study before deciding.

Budget update

The Finance Director reported that revenue is on track and spending is at 16 percent of the annual budget. Fuel costs are higher than planned. No changes to the budget were proposed.

Sidewalk repairs on Spring Street

Public Works reported that 25 sections of sidewalk on Lakeview Road were lifted by tree roots. The estimate to replace them is $43,000. Council member Iris Kaplan asked whether the trees could be kept. Public Works said most could, with root barriers. The council asked for a revised estimate with root barriers included.

Community garden plots

The community garden has 40 plots and a waiting list of 40 households. Council member Lucia Ferreira proposed adding a second garden at the old tennis courts. Staff will report back on water access and cost.

Library roof

The library roof is twenty-six years old and leaked twice this year. Three quotes were received, from $19,000 to $92,000. Council member Lucia Ferreira asked whether the lowest quote included a warranty. It includes ten years. The council voted to accept it.

Parking on Hillcrest Drive

Residents of Fulton Avenue asked for two-hour parking limits during the school year. Council member Desmond Hale noted that the church on the corner relies on street parking on Sundays. The council agreed to limit the rule to weekdays from 7 a.m. to 4 p.m. and to review it after six months.

Public comment

7 residents spoke. Topics included the farmers market and potholes. The Mayor thanked everyone for coming.

The meeting was adjourned at 8:45 p.m.
