# Fox Run: Winter parking rules

From December 1 to March 31, parking on any village street is not allowed between 2 a.m. and 6 a.m. This keeps the streets clear for plowing.

The fine for parking during these hours is $50. During a declared snow emergency, vehicles on the street may also be towed at the owner's cost, whatever the time of day.

Snow emergencies are announced on the village website and by text alert. Sign up for alerts at Village Hall, 77 Commons Drive.
