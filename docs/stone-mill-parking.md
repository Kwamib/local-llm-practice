# Town of Stone Mill: Overnight parking

Overnight parking on residential streets is allowed all year, but only with a residential parking permit displayed in the windshield. Permits cost $30 a year and are sold at the town office on Millrace Road. Each household can buy up to two.

Cars parked overnight without a permit can be fined $75.

Some streets are marked as snow emergency routes with red signs. When the town declares a snow emergency, no one may park on those routes, with or without a permit, until the emergency ends.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
