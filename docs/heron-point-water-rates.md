# City of Heron Point: Water rates for 2027

New water rates take effect on January 1, 2027. Every account pays a base charge of $18 a month, plus $4.10 for every 1,000 gallons used.

Residents aged 65 or older can get a 15% discount on their water bill. The discount is not automatic: fill in the senior discount form at City Hall or on the city website, with proof of age.

Bills are sent every two months. Payments are due 21 days after the bill date.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
