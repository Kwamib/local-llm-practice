# Town of Elm Ridge: Trash and recycling

Trash is collected twice a week, on Mondays and Thursdays. Recycling is collected on Thursdays only.

Elm Ridge does not offer curbside pickup of furniture or other large items. Residents can take them to the Larkspur County transfer station on Route 9, which is open Tuesday to Saturday from 8 a.m. to 4 p.m. The fee is $15 per car or pickup load. Bring proof that you live in Elm Ridge.

Electronics, such as televisions and computers, are not accepted in trash or recycling. The transfer station takes them for free on the second Saturday of each month.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
