# Village of Cedar Bend: Trash and recycling

Household trash is collected every Tuesday. Put your cart at the curb by 6 a.m., with the lid closed and the handle facing your house. Carts left out after 8 p.m. on Wednesday may earn a warning notice.

Recycling is collected every other Friday. The schedule on the back of your recycling calendar shows which Fridays. Rinse containers, flatten boxes, and keep plastic bags out of the recycling cart.

Bulk items, such as furniture, mattresses and appliances, are picked up on the first Saturday of each month. Call Village Hall by the Wednesday before to get on the list. The first three items are free; each item after that costs $25.

Yard waste goes in paper bags, not plastic, and is collected with the trash from April through November.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
