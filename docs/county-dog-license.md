# Larkspur County: Dog licenses

Every dog in Larkspur County must be licensed by the time it is six months old. Licenses are issued by the County Clerk and are valid for one calendar year.

A license costs $12 for a dog that is spayed or neutered, and $20 for a dog that is not. Bring proof of a current rabies vaccination.

Licenses must be renewed every January. A late fee of $5 is added to licenses renewed after January 31.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
