# Willow Bend: Open burning

Burning leaves and brush in your yard needs a burning permit from the Willow Bend Fire Department. Permits are free and last for the whole season.

Open burning is allowed only from October 1 to April 30, between sunrise and sunset. It is never allowed on days the county declares a red flag warning for fire danger.

Only leaves, brush and untreated wood may be burned. Burning trash, plastic or building materials is against the law at any time of year.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
