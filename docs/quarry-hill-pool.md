# Quarry Hill Community Pool

The pool is open from Memorial Day to Labor Day, every day from 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. It closes during thunderstorms and reopens 30 minutes after the last thunder.

A day pass costs $5 for residents and $8 for visitors. A family season pass costs $60 for residents.

Swimming lessons for children run in July. Sign up at the pool office from June 1.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
