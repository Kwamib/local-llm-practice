# Juniper Flats Public Library: Borrowing

Library cards are free for anyone who lives in Larkspur County. People who live outside the county can buy a card for $40 a year.

Books can be borrowed for three weeks and renewed twice, unless someone else has asked for them. DVDs can be borrowed for one week.

The library stopped charging late fines in 2024. Items that are lost or badly damaged are charged at their replacement cost.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
