# Larkspur County: Property tax

Property tax is paid in two halves. The first half is due on March 31, and the second half is due on September 30.

A penalty of 1.5% is added for each month a payment is late.

Homeowners who live in their home can apply for the homestead exemption, which lowers the taxable value of the home. Applications must reach the County Treasurer by December 15 to count for the next year.

This document is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
