# City of Alder Creek: City Council minutes, September 14, 2026

Present: Council President Diego Ramirez, Council members Marcus Bell, Hannah Lindqvist, Ellen Fitzgerald, Kevin Tran and Joyce Abernathy. Absent: Mayor Grace Okafor.
