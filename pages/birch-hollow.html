Town Government

Birch Hollow has a town manager form of government. The five-member Select Board sets policy, and the Town Manager runs day-to-day operations.

Select Board

  • Chair: Margaret Coyle
  • Vice Chair: Owen Pryce
  • Members: Simone Hart, Raj Malhotra, Bill Dorsey

Town Manager

Paul Rinaldi has served as Town Manager since 2021.

Birch Hollow does not have a mayor.
