Election results

Thank you to everyone who voted in the November municipal election.

Rosa Delgado was elected mayor and will be sworn in at the first council meeting in January. Until then, Mayor Tom Haskell continues to serve; he did not seek re-election after two terms.

Council members re-elected: Andre Fontaine and Wendy Liu.

Official results are posted at the Larkspur County Board of Elections.
