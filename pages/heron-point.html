A message from the Mayor

Dear neighbors,

This summer we finished the new boardwalk along the bay, repaved twelve streets, and opened the splash pad at Harbor Park. None of it would have happened without the volunteers who gave their weekends, and the residents who came to our planning meetings.

This fall we begin work on the new fire station. You can follow its progress on the Projects page.

My door at Town Hall is open every Wednesday morning. Please come by.

Warm regards,

Samuel Adeyemi
Mayor of Heron Point

Town of Heron Point, 5 Harbor Road

This town is made up for practice. It is part of the fictional Larkspur County and does not exist.
