Meet your mayor

Mayor Robert "Bob" Chen grew up in Willow Bend and taught science at Willow Bend High School for twenty-two years before joining the City Council in 2019.

Bob was elected mayor in 2023. His priorities are safer crossings near schools, a new community center, and keeping property taxes steady.
