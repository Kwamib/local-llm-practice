Fall Festival returns October 18

The annual Fall Festival returns to Riverside Park on Saturday, October 18, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Expect a pumpkin patch, a craft market with more than forty vendors, live music from local bands, and the famous chili cook-off.

Parking is free at the high school, with shuttle buses running every fifteen minutes. Please leave pets at home, except service animals.

Leaf collection

Curbside leaf collection runs from November 3 to December 12. Rake leaves to the edge of the curb, not into the street, and keep them clear of storm drains.

Road work on Mill Street

Mill Street between 2nd and 5th Avenue will be closed to through traffic from October 6 to October 24 for water main replacement. Local access will be maintained.

Library hours

The Fox Run Public Library is now open until 8 p.m. on Thursdays.
