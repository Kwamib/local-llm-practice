Stone Mill and Cedar Bend agree to share a fire engine

Stone Mill Mayor Ana Silva and Cedar Bend Mayor Dana Whitfield signed an agreement on Monday to share a new ladder truck between the two communities.

"Neither of us could justify one alone," Mayor Silva said. "Together, it covers every building over three stories in both towns."

Mayor Whitfield thanked both fire chiefs for working out the schedule.

The truck arrives in spring.
